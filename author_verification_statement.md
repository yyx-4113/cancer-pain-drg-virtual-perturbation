## Statement

I, the sole author of the above manuscript, verify the following with respect to this
repository.

1. **Provenance of every reported number.** Every quantitative claim in the manuscript,
   its tables and its figure captions is produced by the scripts in `scripts/` and is
   traceable to a deposited artefact under `results/tables/` (CSV or JSON). No number in
   the manuscript was entered by hand from an external source.

2. **Reproducible pass/fail gate.** `scripts/98_check_manuscript_numbers.py` asserts every
   reported number against the deposited artefacts and exits non-zero on any mismatch.
   Its expectations are derived from the artefacts at run time rather than hardcoded. On
   the released tree it reports **0 failed**. `scripts/99_unit_tests.py` asserts the
   statistical and graph primitives and reports **ALL PASSED**.

3. **Data are public and unmodified.** All input data are public GEO datasets
   (GSE149648, GSE160543, GSE292522, GSE249443). They are not redistributed here; GEO is
   the canonical source. No primary data were generated by this study.

4. **Software versions are pinned.** `requirements.txt` records the exact versions used;
   the run aborts rather than silently degrading if a required dependency is absent.

5. **Author identity.** The author holds a **Bachelor of Medicine (B.M.)** only. No
   post-nominal research degree (MD, MS, PhD or equivalent) is claimed anywhere in the
   manuscript, this repository, or the citation metadata.

6. **Conflict of interest.** The author declares no competing interests. The study
   received no specific funding.

7. **Scope of the negative result.** The central claim that in-silico perturbation of
   bulk DRG transcriptomes does not rank analgesic targets is a genuine negative result,
   reported with the negative controls, degree-matched nulls and additive baseline that
   make the failure visible. It is not presented as a positive finding.

8. **Known limitations** are disclosed in the manuscript and summarised in
   `README.md → Caveats and known limitations`.
